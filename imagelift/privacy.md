# ImageLift Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-18

ImageLift does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

- **Page content.** When you click the ImageLift icon, the extension looks for
  the images on the tab you are viewing so it can list them. This happens
  entirely inside your browser.
- **Image files.** When you save images, ImageLift downloads those image files
  from the sites that host them (the same requests your browser makes to show
  the page) and builds the ZIP file on your device. Nothing is sent to us or
  to anyone else.
- **Other sites.** If some images are stored on another site, ImageLift asks
  your permission for that one site before reading its images. You can remove
  that permission at any time in your browser's extension settings.
- **No tracking.** ImageLift contains no analytics, advertising or tracking
  code.
- **Local storage.** The extension stores only its own settings on your device
  (your license key, if you enter one, and a counter of free Pro uses).
- **License check.** If you enter a license key, the key is sent to Gumroad
  (api.gumroad.com), our payment provider, to confirm that it is valid. Nothing
  else is sent. Gumroad's handling of purchase data is covered by Gumroad's own
  privacy policy.
- **Permissions.** `activeTab` and `scripting` are used to find images on the
  current tab after you click the icon. `storage` keeps the settings above.
  Access to `api.gumroad.com` is used only for the license check. Access to
  other sites is optional, asked per site when you click "Allow", and used
  only to read image files of the page you are on.

Questions: contact the developer through the support link on the store listing.
