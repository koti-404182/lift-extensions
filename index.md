# Lift extensions

Small browser extensions for reading and collecting what is on a web page. Everything runs in your browser.

- **SheetLift**: tables to Excel, CSV, Sheets, Markdown, JSON. [Privacy policy](sheetlift/privacy.html)
- **URLLift** (formerly LinkLift): every link on a page to CSV, Excel, Markdown, JSON. [Privacy policy](linklift/privacy.html)
- **ListenLift**: reads articles aloud with your browser's voices, highlighting each sentence. [Privacy policy](listenlift/privacy.html)
