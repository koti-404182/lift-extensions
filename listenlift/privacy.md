# ListenLift Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15

ListenLift does not collect, store or transmit any personal data.

- **Page content.** When you click the ListenLift icon, press its shortcut or
  use its right-click menu, the extension reads the text of the tab you are
  viewing so it can speak it. This happens inside your browser. ListenLift
  never sends page text to us or to anyone else.
- **Voices.** The text is spoken by your browser's built-in speech feature.
  Most voices run on your device. Some browsers also offer voices marked
  "online" (for example Microsoft Edge's natural voices); if you choose one,
  your browser sends the text to its own speech service, under the browser
  maker's privacy policy. ListenLift labels these voices "online" so you can
  avoid them.
- **No tracking.** ListenLift contains no analytics, advertising or tracking
  code, and makes no network requests of its own.
- **Local storage.** The extension stores only your chosen reading speed and
  voice, on your device.
- **Permissions.** `activeTab` and `scripting` let it read the current tab
  only after you ask it to. `storage` keeps the settings above.
  `contextMenus` adds the right-click items. `tts` (Chrome and Edge) lets it
  pass each sentence to the browser's own voices. It has no access to other sites
  or to your browsing history.

Questions: contact the developer through the support link on the store listing.
